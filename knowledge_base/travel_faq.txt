SMART FLIGHT RESERVATION SYSTEM
TRAVEL KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0

========================================
1. FLIGHT BOOKING POLICIES
========================================

Q: How can a passenger book a flight?
A: Passengers can search flights by entering their source city and destination city. They can select an available flight and complete the booking process.

Q: Can I book a flight without available seats?
A: No. A booking can only be confirmed when seats are available.

Q: Can I view my bookings?
A: Yes. Logged-in passengers can view their booking history through their account.

Q: Do I need an account to book a flight?
A: Yes. Passengers must register and log in before making a booking.

========================================
2. BAGGAGE POLICIES
========================================

Q: How much baggage can I carry?
A: Baggage allowances depend on the airline and ticket category. Passengers should check their airline's baggage policy before travelling.

Q: Can I carry hand luggage?
A: Most airlines permit cabin baggage subject to size and weight restrictions.

Q: Are prohibited items allowed in baggage?
A: Dangerous and restricted items are not permitted. Passengers must follow airport security regulations.

========================================
3. CANCELLATION POLICIES
========================================

Q: Can I cancel my flight booking?
A: Passengers can request cancellation of eligible confirmed bookings through the booking management system.

Q: What happens after cancellation?
A: The booking status changes to cancelled, and the seat becomes available for other passengers.

Q: Can I cancel a booking more than once?
A: No. A booking that has already been cancelled cannot be cancelled again.

========================================
4. PAYMENT POLICIES
========================================

Q: Which payment methods are supported?
A: The system supports UPI, card, net banking and cash as simulated payment modes.

Q: Is payment processed through a real payment gateway?
A: No. This project uses simulated payments for demonstration and testing purposes.

Q: Can I pay for a cancelled booking?
A: No. Payments are not accepted for cancelled bookings.

Q: Is duplicate successful payment allowed?
A: No. The system prevents duplicate successful payments for the same booking.

========================================
5. REFUND POLICIES
========================================

Q: How are refunds handled?
A: Refund eligibility and amounts depend on the applicable airline and fare conditions.

Q: Does the project automatically process refunds?
A: No. Automatic refund processing is not currently implemented in this demonstration system.

========================================
6. AIRPORT AND TRAVEL FAQs
========================================

Q: When should I arrive at the airport?
A: Passengers should generally arrive at least two hours before domestic departures and three hours before international departures. Airline and airport instructions may differ.

Q: What documents should I carry?
A: Passengers should carry valid government-issued identification and any required travel documents.

Q: What happens if my flight is delayed?
A: Passengers should check their airline's official updates for the latest departure information.

Q: Can flight schedules change?
A: Yes. Flight schedules are subject to change. Passengers should verify their flight details before departure.

========================================
7. IMPORTANT SYSTEM LIMITATIONS
========================================

- Flight data in this project is sample data.
- The system does not connect to live airline databases.
- Payment processing is simulated.
- Automatic refund processing is not implemented.
- Airline-specific baggage allowances are not stored in the system.
- Passengers should verify official airline policies before travelling.
